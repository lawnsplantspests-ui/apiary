What Camp Hill properties typically call us about

Camp Hill is West Shore established suburb with mature shade trees, pre-war housing, and tight residential lots. The typical bee-related calls we get from the area:

  • Honey bee swarms on mature street trees during swarm season
  • Established colonies in older Camp Hill chimney chases and porch roof voids
  • Bumble bee nests under sheds, decks, and in mulch piles
  • Mistaken-identity calls for yellow jackets in late summer (sister LPP handles)
  • Carpenter bee structural damage on cedar trim (sister LPP)

Why call a beekeeper

We relocate honey bees alive

Honey bees are pollinators worth protecting, so a colony we remove doesn't get killed — it gets relocated. We use bee vacuums that collect the bees alive, save and reuse comb where possible, and the colony goes into one of our hives to keep working. For honey bees, that's almost always a better outcome than a can of spray.

Wasps, hornets, and yellow jackets are a different situation — they're not savable pollinators, and there the right answer is licensed control, not relocation. That's exactly what our sister company Lawns Plants & Pests LLC is for, at the same number, 717-379-3248. One call covers both: honey bees relocated alive, wasps and hornets handled by the licensed crew.
